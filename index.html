2Provenance

The year it had no V8 M3 to face, and the only RS4 with a V8, a manual and a saloon.

  1. Feb 2005Prototype, “quattro Night”, Ingolstadt
  2. Jan 2006Detroit show debut
  3. Mid-2006On sale in Europe
  4. Jun 2006US deliveries begin
  5. Late 2006Cabriolet on sale in Europe
  6. Autumn 2008Production ends

Four years after the B5 RS4 Avant left production, quattro GmbH revived the badge on the B7 A4, and built every car at its Neckarsulm plant. S1 S2

Four things were new. It was the first RS4 with a V8; the B5 had a 2.7-litre twin-turbo V6. It was Audi’s first normally aspirated RS model. It was the first RS4 saloon and the only RS4 Cabriolet, after the Avant-only RS2 and B5. And it brought back a name that had skipped a generation: there was no B6 RS4. S1 S4

BMW’s E46 M3, 343 PS from a straight-six, left production in 2006; the E90 M3 and its 420 PS V8 arrived in 2007. For about a year the RS4 was the class’s only 420 PS high-revving V8, and unlike either M3 it drove all four wheels and came as an estate. S3

Engine bayBNS 4.2 FSI V8 · jobs 01, 03, 04

locate

420PSat 7,800 rpmB5: 380 PS101 PS per litre

Code BNS. Fuel sprayed straight into the chamber cools the charge, and that is what allows 12.5:1 compression without forced induction. It is also why the intake valves carbon up: no fuel washes them (job 01).

Closed-deck two-piece block, 84.5 mm bores at 90 mm spacing, 18.5 mm bank offset. Roller finger followers with hydraulic lash adjustment; sodium-filled, chrome-plated valve stems; 11.0 mm lift.

One gearbox: a six-speed Getrag manual.

DRC dampersAll four corners · job 02

locate

−30mmride height against an A4−40 mm optional2 diagonal pairs

Mechanical, no electronics. In roll or pitch, oil crosses the diagonal through the valve, stiffening the damper that needs support and relieving the other.

With age the pressure drops: low or uneven stance, poor ride, knocks, weeping dampers (job 02). Many cars now run coilovers. Original, working DRC increasingly marks a collector car.

The rest of the sheet

Clutch and gearbox
The gearbox has proven fault-free. Clutches last about 40,000 miles even driven hard; feel for judder on track cars. A failed £23 clutch switch, ≈£150 fitted, kills cruise control and the throttle blip. S8 S3
Ignition and exhaust
Coil packs fail, misfire and can take the catalysts with them. S3
ECU
Early cars’ lumpy idle was cured by a warranty ECU update. Confirm it was done. S8
Timing gear
No systemic chain or camshaft failure is documented for the BNS; cam-follower wear belongs to the 2.0 TFSI. Still investigate any top-end rattle. S8
Body and interior
Stone-chips, paint repairs, the driver’s outer bolster, the armrest catch. No spare wheel: check the sealant canister is in date. S3 S9
Service intervals
Factory schedule unverified.uv-8 Community practice: oil check every 1,000 miles, decarbonisation about every 40,000. S8 S13
